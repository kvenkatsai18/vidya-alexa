Connect school

What Study Buddy can see

  • Classes and teachers
  • Assignments, due dates, and whether they're turned in
  • Tests and quizzes your teachers post
  • Class materials and teacher notes (to turn them into study sets)
  • Your age range and birthday from Google, only to check whether a parent needs to connect (under 13). If so, nothing is kept.

What it will never do

  • Submit, edit, or post anything
  • Do your homework for you (it helps you learn it instead)
  • Share your school data with anyone else

You can disconnect any time by saying "disconnect my school". Synced classes and assignments are deleted when you do.
